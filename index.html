Learn AI Media with Phoebe

Two tracks into a newsroom that still gets believed

Fewer people, less money, more checking, lower trust - all at once. Media leaders surveyed by the Reuters Institute expect search referrals to fall about 43% within three years, a fifth of them expect losses above 75%, and publisher traffic from Google already fell by roughly a third in a year, with the smallest publishers losing around 60%. Meanwhile rising demand for verification is named as one of the defining forces of the year, fewer than four in ten people say they trust the news they see, and fewer than 1% of news images published globally carry any provenance metadata at all. An editor track gives editors-in-chief and media executives the trust problem, the rights position, the newsroom policy and the economics after search. A producer track takes reporters and desk staff through one running story at the Harbour Ledger - a port expansion and its 40% emissions claim - with a live verification desk on the key pages where you watch a night of inbound climb from 1 out of 10 to 10 out of 10, and find out why the sixth toggle makes it worse.

The producer track 🗞️ · for reporters, producers & desk editors · 10 x 45 min · one running story

Hands-on mode: you work at the Harbour Ledger, 40 staff, a site, a daily newsletter, a weekly podcast and a video desk of two. The running story is the port expansion and its 40% emissions claim - a press release, a careful filing, a leaked spreadsheet, a disputed satellite image and a very confident spokesperson. Ten sessions take it from a tip to a published story in three formats with its provenance intact.
